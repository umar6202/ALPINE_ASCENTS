MOUNTAINEERING

A Journey Beyond the Ordinary

Mountaineering is the sport of attaining, or attempting to attain, high points in mountainous regions. It combines physical challenge, preparation, experience and respect for the mountain environment.

From historic expeditions to modern climbing techniques, every ascent represents a combination of knowledge, preparation and determination.

01 / HISTORY

The History of Mountaineering

Mountaineering has developed through generations of exploration and climbing in mountainous regions. Early expeditions gradually developed the skills, knowledge and experience needed to travel through difficult mountain terrain.

Over time, mountaineering became more organized, with expeditions, climbing communities and established approaches to mountain exploration.

From exploration to modern climbing
02 / TYPES & STYLES

Different Ways to Climb

Mountaineering includes different climbing styles and approaches depending on the terrain, mountain conditions and objectives of an expedition.

Climbers may encounter rock, snow, ice and mixed terrain. Each environment requires different preparation and techniques.

04 / SHELTERING

Shelter in the Mountains

Sheltering is an important part of mountain expeditions. Mountain environments can change quickly, making suitable shelter an essential consideration during an expedition.

Depending on the environment and expedition, climbers may use established mountain shelters, tents or other suitable forms of protection from the surrounding conditions.

Mountain environments can present serious challenges. Understanding the surrounding conditions is an important part of mountaineering.

Extreme Weather

Avalanches

High Altitude

Difficult Terrain
